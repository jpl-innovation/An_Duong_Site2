ADDC WEBSITE: THE GOAL FOR ANY WORK ON THE SITE ITSELF (read before changing pages, motion or 3D wiring)

Site folder: D:\Rum website\ctyanduong\ctyanduong (branch v2; live at jpl-innovation.github.io/An_Duong_Site2).

The owner's benchmark is these award-winning sites. Study them before designing anything:
- https://www.maman-corp.com/ : the best for creativity and smoothness. Logo-led loader with a real 0-100 count,
  a panel lifting to reveal a full-screen photo, eased motion everywhere, nothing jumps or flashes.
- https://endcap.design/ : headlines rising line by line out of a mask; confident, restrained motion.
- https://www.skanska.com/us/en : sideways image strips driven by the vertical scroll.
- https://www.mccarthy.com/ : an impressive first screen.
- https://suffolk.com/ : the "how we build" section.
- Also: turnerconstruction.com, citadelgroup.org, bpmarineconstruction.com, havenconstructions.com.au,
  montarville.ca, rapidkert.com.

Non-negotiables:
1. Smooth: no frame longer than 50 ms while scrolling on a 240 Hz screen; 3D drawn every frame, never capped at 60.
   Models are downloaded ahead (preloadModels), parsed one per frame, and their textures uploaded a few per frame
   (warmModel in js/scene3d.js) before they are first shown. Never add work to the frame a model appears in.
2. No flashes: pictures start loading two screens ahead; picture frames wait in a dark tone, never a pale card.
3. Loading screens use the ADDC house mark (brandMark in js/app.js): house lines, the logo square rising with real
   progress, the wordmark sliding out of the wall. Counts are real, never a timer pretending to load.
4. Creative but clean: avoid the 20 AI-slop signs (tags and pills, eyebrows, ALL CAPS subtitles, section numbering,
   gradient backgrounds, bordered cards, excessive mono or italics, periods in titles, and the rest).
5. Real ADDC work photos only; no family photos.
6. Check everything before it goes live: every page at 2460 px and 390 px, frame timing, console errors.
7. Commits carry no Claude or AI co-author or session lines: all credit goes to the owner of this repository.
